# Verk — Brand Strategy

---

## Reference Findings Used

- **Basic/Dept**: primary positioning reference — polished competence, direct brand voice, confident studio framing
- **Instrument**: how a tech-led agency positions as a premium partner rather than a vendor
- **Bakken & Bæck**: restraint in language, letting the work speak before the words
- **Studio Freight**: the framing of a studio that builds things, not a studio that talks about building things

---

## What Verk Is

Verk is a digital infrastructure studio.

It builds the operational systems that modern businesses need to attract clients, capture leads, automate follow-up, and run their digital presence with precision.

Specifically, Verk builds:

- High-performance websites (not just designed — engineered)
- Lead capture systems connected to real business workflows
- Business automation (CRM, WhatsApp, form + email flows)
- AI-powered internal tools and client-facing workflows
- Technical SEO foundations
- Custom integrations (CRM, WhatsApp, calendars, payments)
- Digital products (SaaS prototypes, directories, internal tools)
- Operational infrastructure for businesses replacing manual processes

Verk's output is not a deliverable handed over in a ZIP file. It's a system that works while the client sleeps.

## Target Clients

### Primary (V1 Market — León, Bajío, México)

- **Clínicas y consultorios médicos**: estética, odontología, dermatología, especialidades. Need lead capture, appointment flows, WhatsApp integration, fast professional site.
- **Despachos de arquitectura**: need portfolio + project systems that reflect their craft level.
- **Inmobiliarias y constructoras**: need lead funnels, property listing systems, WhatsApp flows, fast load on mobile.
- **Firmas de servicios profesionales**: contadores, abogados, consultores. Need credibility online + client-facing systems.
- **Negocios locales con volumen**: restaurants, retailers, clinics with repeat clients — need automation to stop losing leads.

### Secondary (V1.5 — Regional & Remote)

- B2B companies across Mexico needing operational digital infrastructure
- Startups needing an MVP digital presence and lead system
- Companies that have already tried agencies and gotten a generic result

### Future (V2)

- International clients (English market)
- Companies seeking AI-powered internal tools
- Businesses needing custom digital products

## Core Business Problem Verk Solves

Most businesses in the primary target market face the same situation:

1. Their website is slow, ugly, or irrelevant — or doesn't exist in a useful form
2. Leads arrive on WhatsApp and get lost in conversation threads
3. Follow-up is manual and inconsistent
4. There is no connection between their digital presence and their actual operation
5. They've hired "agencies" before and received a beautiful PDF and a slow website that no one maintains
6. Their competitors are catching up digitally and they're still on the same template from 2018

The result: businesses that work hard offline but lose potential clients at every digital touchpoint.

Verk solves this by building the complete digital infrastructure layer: site + capture + automation + integration.

## Tone of Voice

### Write like this:

- "Tu sitio debería trabajar mientras tú duermes."
- "Automatizamos el seguimiento que nunca haces porque no tienes tiempo."
- "Menos WhatsApps perdidos. Más clientes atendidos."
- "Un sitio web que no captura leads es un gasto, no una inversión."
- "Construimos infraestructura, no presentaciones."

### Never write like this:

- "Soluciones digitales integrales"
- "Lleva tu negocio al siguiente nivel"
- "Transformamos tu presencia digital"
- "Diseño web profesional y de calidad"
- "Innovación para tu empresa"
- "Marketing digital 360"
- "Equipo apasionado de creativos"
- "Nos dedicamos a hacer tus sueños realidad"

### Voice characteristics:

- **Direct**: State the problem before offering the solution
- **Specific**: Reference real pain points (WhatsApp groups, Excel sheets, slow sites)
- **Confident without arrogance**: No boasting, just clarity
- **Technical but human**: Use system language without alienating non-technical clients
- **Premium**: Short sentences. No filler. Every word earns its place.

## Brand Identity Notes (from logo inspection)

### Full Logo (`logo.png`)
Black lowercase "verk" wordmark in a bold, wide, slightly rounded geometric sans-serif. Two diagonal parallelogram shapes in lime-green (#BFEA00 approx) cross through the letterforms, creating visual dynamism. Tagline below in small caps, tracked wide: **CONECTAMOS. AUTOMATIZAMOS. CONSTRUIMOS.**

The tagline is the brand strategy in three words. Use it.

### Simple Logo (`logo-simple.png`)
The wordmark alone on warm cream. For contexts where the geometric shapes would create visual noise.

### Isotipo (`isotipo.png`)
The two parallelogram shapes rendered in blue-violet (#6B6AF4 approx), without the wordmark. An abstract symbol suggesting motion, connectivity, and infrastructure — not a gear, node, circuit, or generic tech icon.

The isotipo changes color between the full logo (lime) and the standalone mark (violet). This color flexibility should be used intentionally in the visual system.
